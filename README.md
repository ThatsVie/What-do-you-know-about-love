## Project Overview

_What Do You Know About Love?_ was a digital platform exploring love through personal reflections and AI-generated imagery.  
By pairing deeply human stories with AI visuals, the project invited audiences to consider how technology mediates love, emotion, and authenticity.

Participants selected a question about love, submitted a response, and saw their words transformed into response cards—each accompanied by an image intended to provoke reflection on perception and meaning.

📖 **Read the closure note:** [Project Closure · What Do You Know About Love?](https://whatdoyouknowaboutlove.com/closure/)

---

## Closure Summary

As of **October 2025**, the project has officially concluded.  
Submissions are closed, and AI-generated imagery is no longer used.

This decision reflects a shift in perspective. While AI once served as a conceptual tool for exploring emotional authenticity, its continued use no longer aligned with the project’s core values due to concerns over uncredited labor, environmental costs, and creative integrity.

The website remains online as a public archive honoring all who contributed and the questions that inspired them.

## Features and Functionality

- **Interactive Response Cards:** Displayed anonymous reflections with “Read More” expanders for accessibility.  
- **Search and Question Filters:** Allowed browsing by theme or keyword.  
- **Smooth Navigation:** Included scroll controls and responsive design.  
- **Alt Text & Accessibility:** Every image and card was described for inclusivity.

## Submission Workflow

- **Google Forms** – Collected anonymous submissions securely.  
- **Google Sheets** – Stored and organized responses.  
- **Apps Script + Manual Curation** – Combined automation and human review to ensure clarity and ethical presentation.

## Imagery and Visuals

Early in development, _What Do You Know About Love?_ used AI-generated images (DALL-E) to accompany human reflections on love.  
This pairing was designed to highlight contrasts between emotion and automation, story and simulation.

Over time, the project acknowledged the ethical complexities of that choice recognizing that creativity rooted in care and credit matters more than technological novelty.

---

## Evolving Ethics & Reflection

- The project originally employed AI imagery as a mirror for questioning authenticity.  
- Later, its creator chose to end AI use to align with principles of transparency, sustainability, and justice.  
- This evolution is detailed in the [Closure Statement](https://whatdoyouknowaboutlove.com/closure/), which marks a transition from experimentation toward integrity-driven creation.

> “Closing this project is an act of accountability and care.”  
> — *Vie P., October 2025*
